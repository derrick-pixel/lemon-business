Economics 101

A "lemon" is the economic term for a bad deal you can't spot in advance.

Nobel laureate George Akerlof used it for used cars: when buyers can't tell good from bad, the market fills with lemons and the peaches get squeezed out.

Extending credit or signing a contract is the exact same market. A reliable counterparty and a serial late-payer look identical on a quotation. You only find out which one you signed after the invoice is already overdue — and your cashflow is already on the line.

Lemon Business fixes the information gap. We don't make your commercial decisions for you. We just make sure the lemon can't hide.
