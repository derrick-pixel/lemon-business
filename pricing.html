Appeals

Appeals are free. For everyone.

A business that disputes an upheld record can file a free independent appeal — reviewed by a panel that isn't the original reporter. No fee, no fast-track tier, no priced jump-the-queue option.

Lemon Business does not monetise removals. The platform makes money on lookups and uploads; correcting a record never carries a price tag.

Self-check

Filing more than 10 lemons a quarter? Declare your trade volume.

A business filing a pile of disputes isn't always catching lemons — sometimes it's the hard one to deal with. To keep uploading past the quarterly threshold, you declare your trade volume. The maths has to make sense, or the board reads you as the difficult counterparty everyone else is avoiding.
